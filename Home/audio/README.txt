Recorded cues (mono MP3, 96 kbps, ≤ 300 KB together) drop in here by filename and
are listed in manifest.json, e.g. ["sleeve-out.mp3", "needle-drop.mp3"]. Anything not
listed plays the procedural placeholder from synth.js. Names: ui-press-down,
ui-press-up, jacket-pick, jacket-shift, sleeve-out, record-set, tonearm-lift,
tonearm-swing, needle-drop, play-press, spin-loop (seamless ≥ 4 s loop).
