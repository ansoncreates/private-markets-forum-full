Variant B

Ranked bar, label above.

Same markup as variant A. The only change is chart__rows--stacked on the rows container, so tooltips, focus order and the reveal all carry over untouched.

Label sits above the bar at body size. The figure takes the display serif at 24/32, right-aligned on the label's baseline. The bar drops to 6px with pill caps, filled with --data-1 — #133EEC on light plates — over the lightest step of the same hue, --data-s1.
